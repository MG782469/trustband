    What the full system is designed to do

    Proposed behaviour, shown for illustration only. These outputs are not measured results.

    SupportedEnough independent stories and calibration data: show the forecast with its interval.
    Fragile evidenceThe forecast hangs on one story: lean on the price-only model and adjust the interval.
    Insufficient supportUnreliable timestamps or too little calibration data: make no confident call.

    Data: FNSPID (KDD 2024), research use. Models: LightGBM on lagged returns, volatility and volume, plus FinBERT headline sentiment. Intervals: split conformal on a 2018–19 calibration window. Fallback: if removing one story moves the forecast by more than the validation threshold, or the day has fewer than two stories, the price-only model is used.
